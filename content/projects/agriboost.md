## The problem
Sugarcane farmers lose yield to leaf diseases that are caught late, and fertilizer is often applied without knowing the state of the soil.

## What I built
- **Disease detection.** Trained CNNs that diagnose sugarcane leaf diseases from a photo and suggest actions.
- **Soil intelligence.** IoT sensors stream pH, moisture and temperature; Random Forest classifiers turn readings into fertilizer recommendations.
- **Platform.** Django APIs for inference and sensor processing, and a real-time React dashboard backed by Firebase.
